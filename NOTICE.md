# Exhibition image provenance

The exhibition at `/exhibition/` reuses six photographs under open licences.
Each is credited in its on-page caption, generated from `_data/shelves.yml`.
Licence terms differ, so keep the caption accurate if an image is swapped.

Five files below were matched to their source by MD5 checksum: the file in this
repository is byte-for-byte identical to the file at the URL given. All were
taken at the 1280px Wikimedia Commons rendition.

### `assets/images/dwarf-figure.jpg`

*Statuette of a dancing dwarf.* Ptolemaic Period, 332-150 BCE. Marble, H 10 cm.
The Metropolitan Museum of Art, accession 26.7.1403, Purchase, Edward S.
Harkness Gift, 1926. **CC0.**

- Museum record: <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/551343>
- File: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dancing_dwarf_MET_DP238936.jpg>

Note: the on-page caption currently reads "Hellenistic or Roman". The Met
catalogues this object as Ptolemaic Period, in its Egyptian Art department. The
caption and the shelf text need review against the museum record.

### `assets/images/takhos-sarcophagus.jpg` - source not yet verified

A historic photographic print. The caption written into the negative reads
"Sarkophage of the dwarf Takhos (Ptolem. periode)" and "Cairo Museum", numbered
793. Credited here as Museo Egizio photographic archive, Turin (CC0), but that
attribution has **not been verified and no permanent link has been found.**

This is the one outstanding item in this record. Likely source: the Museo
Egizio digital archive, <https://collezioni.museoegizio.it/>. Retrace the
download, record the link, and confirm the licence before publication.
